## Maintenance / packaging / data limits

- Cleanup is opt-in preview + confirmation only. Backup integrity/hash, rollback,

  Local ingestion is atomically excluded during explicit cleanup. An unrelated
  external writer during backup causes a no-change abort, and a fresh explicit
  retry backs up the newly added row before any merge.
